Helping the boys each week

You don’t need to know the answers. Your job is rhythm and nudges.

Wednesday after the session
Ask each of them: what’s your project, and what’s the first step? Their hubs have it in writing.
Midweek check
Ten minutes each. What have you tried, where are you stuck? Stuck is fine, it’s what we talk about next session.
Tuesday night
Help them film a 1 to 2 minute video and post it in the WhatsApp group. Presenting is half the learning.
Let them drive
Keep your hands off the keyboard. If they ask you how, ask them what they’d tell Claude.

How each week works

Same rhythm every week, so you always know what’s next. We’ll shape the projects around what you’re into.

Karl teaches for about 20 minutes, then sets the week’s project.
During the week
Work on your project with Claude. Getting stuck is part of it: write down what was hard.
Tuesday night
Record a 1 to 2 minute phone video: what you built, what worked, what was hard. Post it in the WhatsApp group.
Back on Wednesday

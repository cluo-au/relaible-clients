What we need from you

Everything that only you can give us

  1. Install day. Be there for the logins only you can approve, like the Mac mini's admin password and Apple ID, and let us use your office internet for the big downloads. Wed 14 Oct, 12 to 3pm.
  2. Pick the voice. An offline voice keeps everything on the Mac mini. Microsoft's voice sounds a little better, but the words it speaks are sent to Microsoft to make the audio. We suggest offline. Wed 14 Oct.
  3. Redacted examples in the AI project folder: 2 or 3 past credit papers, loan approval documents, your portfolio sheet with the WIP tab, and your CRM sheet. By Fri 23 Oct.
  4. 2 or 3 short Q&A sessions, around 30 minutes each, on how you put a credit paper together and the terms you use. Week of 19 Oct.
  5. A live deal to test on, and your mark-up of the first draft. By Fri 30 Oct.
  6. The payment dashboard: who should see it, and whether you'd also like a short summary email. By Fri 6 Nov.
  7. Use it on real work for a week and tell us what's off. Mon 9 to Thu 12 Nov.
